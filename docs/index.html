Featured research

Independent research manuscript · October 2026

How Strong Is the Assurance?

Verification and the Limits of Independent AI Safety Evaluation. A targeted literature synthesis examining evaluated-to-deployed system correspondence through five layers: artifact, runtime, behavior, safeguards, and continuity.

The paper proposes a claim-specific correspondence statement for reporting evidence, trust dependencies, unsupported claims, and changes that may invalidate an evaluation. The framework is conceptual and has not been validated on deployed frontier models.
